The following table lists the emoji that can be entered via tab completion of their \:name: abbreviations in the Julia REPL (and in various other editing environments). You can also get information on how to type an emoji by entering it in the REPL help, i.e. by typing ? and then entering the emoji in the REPL (e.g., by copy-paste from somewhere you saw the emoji).

For the LaTeX-like abbreviations that complete to mathematical symbols and other non-emoji characters, see Unicode Input.
